Protect yourself from job scams

  • Never pay someone to secure an interview or job.
  • Do not share bank PINs, passwords or one-time codes.
  • Check the employer's official website and email domain independently.
  • Be careful with requests for money, gift cards or cryptocurrency.
  • For government jobs, use official recruitment portals and notices.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for Zimbabwean job seekers.

Are all jobs on this page verified?

These listings are linked to official employer or government career pages and were checked on the date shown. This does not guarantee that a vacancy remains open or that every detail is unchanged. The original notice is the authority.

Do I apply through Zim Jobs Daily?

No. This page is an independent job-information directory. Open the official source link and follow the employer's instructions there.

Why does a listing say “internal advert”?

An internal advert may be intended only for existing employees or another restricted group. Read the official notice carefully before spending time on an application.

Does this site charge job seekers?

No. Browsing and saving listings on this static page is free. Never pay a third party who claims they can guarantee employment.
